Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about booking a consultation with Vantage Education.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes, absolutely! The initial consultation is completely free with no obligation. We want you to have the opportunity to learn about our services and see if we're a good fit for your needs before making any commitment.

How long does the consultation take?

The free consultation typically lasts 30 minutes. This gives us enough time to understand your goals, answer your questions, and provide initial insights while respecting your time.

What should I prepare for the consultation?

You don't need to prepare anything specific, but it's helpful to think about your academic goals, target universities or countries you're interested in, and any questions you'd like to ask. Having your academic background information handy can also be useful.

How quickly will you contact me after I submit the form?

We aim to contact you within 24 hours of receiving your form submission. Our team will reach out via email or phone to schedule your consultation at a time that's convenient for you.

Can I book a consultation if I'm not sure which service I need?

Absolutely! That's exactly what the consultation is for. We'll help you understand your options, assess your needs, and recommend the best services for your situation. You don't need to know which service you want before booking.

What happens after the consultation?

After the consultation, you'll have a clear understanding of how we can help you. If you decide to work with us, we'll create a personalized plan and get started. If not, there's no obligation—you'll still have valuable insights to guide your journey.

Do you offer consultations for parents as well?

Yes! We welcome parents to join the consultation. We understand that parents play an important role in the decision-making process, and we're happy to address their questions and concerns as well.

Can I reschedule my consultation?

Of course! We understand that schedules can change. Simply contact us, and we'll be happy to reschedule your consultation for a time that works better for you.
