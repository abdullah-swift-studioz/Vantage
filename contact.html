Often Asked

Common questions about our admissions services.

How far in advance should I start the application process?

We recommend starting at least 12-18 months before your intended start date. This gives ample time for standardized testing, essay drafting, and strategic profile building.

Do you guarantee admission to Ivy League schools?

No ethical counselor can guarantee admission to specific universities. However, our track record shows that 94% of our students are accepted into one of their top 3 choice institutions.

Do you help with student visas?

Yes! We provide comprehensive visa support, including document checks, interview preparation, and post-approval transition planning for all major study destinations.
