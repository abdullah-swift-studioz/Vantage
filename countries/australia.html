Why Choose Australia?

Australia is famous for its laid-back lifestyle, but its education system is rigorous and world-class. It is a leader in research, particularly in sciences and engineering.

Post-Study Work Stream

Graduates can stay and work for 2–4 years depending on their qualification.

High Minimum Wage

Australia has one of the highest minimum wages in the world, making part-time work very lucrative for students.

Regional Benefits

Studying in designated regional areas can grant you extra years on your post-study work visa.
